It seems to me that new knowledge learned from "real" human crafted blogs, articles, or tutorials are getting less and less with the emergence of LLM providers (both open and closed-source), e.g. StackOverflow. What this means is, we are becoming more dependent on AI distilled knowledge, which implies that all the information and knowledge we can access and ask for is constrained by AI provider's whitelist.

One day, access to these information might require you to subscribe/pay to the provider, or even verify your identity for "dangerous" materials. This will concurrently happen alongside the fact that "human generated" materials are getting less and less as well.

With this in mind, I believe we should learn as much knowledge or whatever you think might be impossible to learn if this scenario happens.
